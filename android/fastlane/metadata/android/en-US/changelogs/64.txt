Settings has a Theme section with Appearance and Audio. Audio offers Classic or Crow sounds for calls and new messages.
Classic uses Caw's own ringtone and message sound instead of the phone's defaults. A custom sound or Do Not Disturb override set for Caw's notifications has to be set again.
A tone plays when a call ends.
With an up-to-date Caw server, devices that catch up after someone left a group, or was removed, still show that member's earlier messages.
